Sean Parabuac

Sean Parabuac, penname "DarkVoid", is a small fantasy and fanfiction author specializing in the complexities of the conflict between intelligences. These intelligences refer to mindsets coming from people with different backgrounds that make up the foundation of their thinking processes. Debates and arguments are often depicted with people that hugely contrast from each other from a personality standpoint.

Currently operating using Wattpad, Sean creates works with thorough research, often equipped with great source material and an impressive understanding of the situation in-universe. This ability is seen throughout most of his work and can be seen as a trademark, a specialty that the author has mastered as he continues his writing journey. Though his life situation prevents him from staying consistent, active supporters have constantly expressed their joy in reading Sean's work.

Over the course of his writing career, Sean's ability to immerse his readers from the first word to the last has improved by leaps and bounds. Upon closer inspection, he hides small pieces of information in one chapter that becomes a crucial piece of evidence in another. The more perceptive readers have observed this writing behavior and are surprised yet impressed by how the author urges you to pay attention to all the content he posts.
