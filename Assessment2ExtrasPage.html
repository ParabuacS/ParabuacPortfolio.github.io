The following are works that have either been unpublished or
are currently undergoing preparations to be shared at a later date.

Ode To Eternity

An original story following a small group of people in the war torn world of Somidei. As incidents started popping up lefty and right, they have to investigate the root cause before another war befalls them once more. Whether they succeed or not is another story entirely.



The Mysterious Heroes

When random individuals showed up to protect the Frostbourne, it was clear they had an ulterior motif. Just as the Forsbourne investigated, their enemies grew in number, and now they have to ally with each other to defend themselves.



The Eight Mysteries

When the world needed them no longer, they disappeared just as they showed up. Now, the Eight Mysteries were called by the gods once more to protect the world from a returning evil. Will they answer the call, or disappear entirely?
